# Image credits

The prototype uses openly licensed stand-in photography. All images are cropped and resized (`assets/images/`). The footer does not show these credits, at the client's request.

**Before publishing,** do one of the following:
- add a visible credits page or link: CC BY and CC BY-SA licences require attribution in a reasonable place on the site;
- replace the photos with commissioned or licensed brand photography.

The Creative Commons licences do not cover the personality rights of identifiable people, the child in the medical photo for example. Get model releases before using such images in production.
